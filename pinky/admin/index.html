For a smooth professional 360° view, place the item on a turntable (or turn the mannequin a little each time) and take 24 to 36 photos in one full circle, same distance and light. Select them all here. Fewer than 24 photos look jerky. Up to 60 are allowed.

Categories

Category = a tab at the top of the website (Sarees, Kurtis…). Folder = a card inside a category that groups similar items (same name). Under every category you can see its folders and add items into them.

How discounts and coupons work

Item discount: in the Collection tab, fill MRP and Selling price. The website shows the cut price, the old price struck out, the % off and how much the customer saves. The item stays in its normal category with the cut price. It goes into the "Discounts & offers" section only if you choose it (below, or by ticking the box in the Collection form).

Coupon: a code you create here and give to special customers. When a customer types it on the website, they see an extra discount on every price. The customer then messages you on WhatsApp with the code, and you give the discount at the shop. Online payment is not part of this website, so please check the code yourself.
